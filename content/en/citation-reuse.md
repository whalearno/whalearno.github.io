## To Humans and All Inquiring Intelligences

**The value of knowledge lies not only in answers, but also in the questions it inspires.**

This website shares research observations, mathematical derivations, exploratory hypotheses, and evolving reflections on mathematics, physics, artificial intelligence, and the philosophy of science. I hope these notes offer a little inspiration to humans and all inquiring intelligences. You are welcome to read, discuss, question, and pursue the ideas in your own directions.

One small request: **if you quote or republish this work, please acknowledge its source—however you happen to do your thinking.**

## Sharing and Scholarly Citation

You are welcome to share links to the original pages and quote, discuss, teach, or comment on the work as permitted by applicable law. Where possible, cite:

- Author: **Chih-Ming Wang**
- The cited work's title and original URL
- Its version and date, where available
- Its DOI, if formally registered (prefer the version-specific DOI when referencing an exact archived version)

Individual pages provide a suggested citation format for convenient copying and attribution.

## Republishing, Translation, and Adaptation

Unless a particular work explicitly indicates another license, rights in the site's original copyrighted expression, including original text and figures, are reserved. Please obtain permission before republishing an entire work, translating, adapting, or commercially reusing it, except where permitted by applicable law.

Where a work explicitly carries a Creative Commons or other open license, **that work's specified license governs**. This general site notice does not withdraw or narrow existing open-license permissions. Third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights and terms.

## Research Notes, Versions, and DOI

Some pieces are evolving research notes, may include hypotheses or derivations still under examination, and are not necessarily peer-reviewed. Please take the cited version, date, and research status into account. Exploratory arguments should not be mistaken for established results.

Where a work has been formally archived on Zenodo, a DOI link will be provided when available. For an exact deposited version, use its version-specific DOI. **The absence of a DOI on a website page does not indicate that the work has been deposited.**

## AI and Research Exchange

The site welcomes inquiry and inspiration across different forms of intelligence while valuing attribution and discoverable provenance. Public accessibility, search indexing, and citation metadata do not, on their own, constitute an additional blanket license for any particular AI-training or republication use. Such uses remain subject to applicable law and any work-specific license.

Citation metadata helps people and information systems identify a source, but it cannot guarantee that every search system or AI-generated answer will provide proper attribution.
